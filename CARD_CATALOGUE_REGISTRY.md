# Master Registry: Indian Credit Card Catalogue & Official Webpages

> **Purpose & Lifecycle Tracking:**  
> This registry compiles the official product catalogues, Most Important Terms & Conditions (MITC), schedule of charges/tariffs, and revision/devaluation notification pages for **all credit card issuing institutions in India** (Public Sector Banks, Private Banks, Foreign Banks, Small Finance Banks, and RBI-regulated Co-branded Fintech programs).
>
> *Use this registry to monitor card feature changes, reward rate devaluations, annual fee changes, milestone revisions, and decommissioned/delisted cards.*

---

## Why MITC & Tariff Pages Matter for Tracking Changes
Under the **RBI Master Direction – Credit Card and Debit Card – Issuance and Conduct Directions**:
1. **30-Day Mandatory Notice:** Issuers must give cardholders at least 30 days' advance notice before altering fees, charges, reward structures, or terms.
2. **MITC as Legal Source of Truth:** Marketing pages often lag behind changes. The **MITC (Most Important Terms and Conditions)** and **Schedule of Charges** documents hosted on the links below are the legally binding source of truth for lounge access criteria, surcharge waivers, forex markup, reward capping, and exclusions (e.g., fuel, rent, wallet, utility).

## 8. Card Status Monitoring: Devaluation & Decommissioned Checklist

When auditing the webpages above to detect changes, look out for the following 6 recurring industry patterns:

1. **Spend-Based Lounge Criteria:**  
   *Trend:* Banks transitioning from unconditioned complimentary lounge access to minimum quarterly spends (e.g., ICICI ₹35,000/qtr, Axis ₹50,000/3-months, HDFC ₹50,000/qtr).
2. **Category Exclusions:**  
   *Trend:* Zero reward points on **Government transactions, Utility bill payments, Insurance premiums, School/College fees, Fuel, Rent, and Wallet reloads**.
3. **Reward Redemption Capping & Fees:**  
   *Trend:* Introduction of redemption processing fees (typically ₹99 + GST per request) and monthly conversion limits to air miles/hotel points.
4. **Dynamic & Tiered Forex Markup:**  
   *Trend:* Shifting from static markup (e.g., 3.5%) to differential forex charges or surcharge addition on DCC (Dynamic Currency Conversion) overseas transactions.
5. **Decommissioned & Migrated Cards:**  
   *Examples:*  
   - Citi India cards migrated into Axis Bank equivalents (e.g., PremierMiles $\to$ Axis Horizon/Miles & More).  
   - HDFC JetPrivilege / InterMiles cards sunsetted.  
   - Vistara co-branded cards across Axis, SBI Card, and Club Vistara IDFC transitioning into Air India Flying Returns programs following the airline merger.  
   - Standard Chartered DigiSmart & Manhattan phased out for newer products.
6. **BMS & Movie Ticket Changes:**  
   *Trend:* Replacing flat 1+1 movie offers with capped discounts (e.g., "Up to ₹250 off on 2nd ticket" instead of free 2nd ticket).
